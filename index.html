1. Executive FTO Clearance (vs. US12377920B1)

Independent Claim 1 and Claim 11 of US12377920B1 require a mandatory dual-configuration skirt sequence wherein a second operating mode deploys side skirts while retracting front and rear skirts during active fan operation. The Roadiris™ architecture operates via a unified peripheral seal loop and achieves millisecond vacuum modulation purely via internal fluidic bypass routing — completely omitting the limiting multi-mode skirt element under the All-Elements Rule.

2. Thermodynamic Synergy & Retrofit Viability

At ΔP = 0.12 bar across a 1.8 m² underbody plenum, 21.6 kN of vertical suction force directly pre-loads the chassis energy accumulator during normal driving. Upon obstacle detection, vacuum lock is broken in <15 ms without rotor deceleration, while fan exhaust pre-charges the high-pressure compressor and augments triple-stream ejector thrust.
